Privacy policy

In effect from November 23, 2026. It covers the Hunch app for iPhone and this website, playhunch.co.

The short version. You can play Hunch without an account, and then we don’t know who you are. If you sign in, we keep your sign-in details and your game record so you can get them back on another phone. We don’t show ads, we don’t sell or rent your information, and the app has no advertising or tracking software in it. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who we are

Hunch is made and run by Morgan Brown, an independent developer in the United States. “We” in this policy means Hunch. For anything about your privacy, write to privacy@playhunch.co.

What we collect

If you play without an account

Your progress, settings and detective’s card stay on your phone. We also receive two things:

  • Gameplay events. The app sends a record of how cases are played: which case and difficulty, the scenes you reach, the choices you make, the clues you find, how long puzzles take, who you accuse and how the case ends. These carry a random identifier created on your phone when the app is installed, the app version, and the word “ios”. They don’t carry your name, phone number, email address or Apple account. We use them to find and fix scenes and puzzles that are confusing or unfair.
  • Feedback you choose to send. If you flag a line, rate a puzzle or a case, or write a note, we receive what you wrote and where in the case you were. If you type a name into the optional name box, we receive that too.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. If you do, we also store:

We never receive your Apple or Google password. Hunch has no passwords of its own.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your location, your contacts, your photos, or Apple’s advertising identifier. We don’t track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Our server sees your IP address when the app connects, as every server does, but we don’t store it with your gameplay events.

This website

playhunch.co sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The company that hosts it may keep standard server logs, which include IP addresses, for a short time.

Messages we send

  • Sign-in codes. If you sign in with a phone number, or add and confirm a phone number or email address, we send a one-time code by text or email. Message and data rates may apply to texts.
  • Everything else is off until you turn it on. Daily case reminders, streak reminders, email news and texts each have their own switch in Settings, and all of them start switched off. You can switch any of them off again at any time.

We don’t sell your information and we don’t share it for advertising. We would hand information over only if the law required it, or to protect someone’s safety.

How long we keep it

  • Account information is kept until you delete your account.
  • Gameplay events and feedback are kept while they are useful for improving the cases. They are filed under a random install identifier, not under your account.

Deleting your information

  • Delete your account in the app. Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your account and every row stored with it, and tells Apple to withdraw Hunch’s access if you signed in with Apple. It can’t be undone.
  • Reset your progress. Settings also has Reset progress, which clears your record and keeps your account.
  • Delete gameplay events or feedback. Write to privacy@playhunch.co. Because these aren’t filed under your name, tell us roughly when you played or what you wrote so we can find them.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to privacy@playhunch.co and we will answer within 30 days. We won’t treat you differently for asking.

How we protect it

Information travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections. Each account can read and change only its own rows in the database. Your sign-in session is kept in the iPhone’s Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, so if something goes wrong that affects your information, we will tell you.

Children

Hunch’s cases are murder mysteries and are not written for children. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and change the date at the top. If the change is a significant one and you have an account, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
